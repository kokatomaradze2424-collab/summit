Climb the tower. One swing at a time.

Summit is a physics climbing game for iPhone. You are a man in a copper pot with a pickaxe. Hook ledges, pull yourself up and climb a skyscraper under construction, ten chapters from the street to the crown.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Write to kokabasket@hotmail.com. We answer within a few days.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the chapter you were on.

FAQ

How do I play? Drag your finger to move the pickaxe. Hook it on an edge and push to lift the pot.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Do I need an internet connection? No. The game works fully offline.

Restoring a purchase: the game is a one-time purchase. Redownload it from the App Store with the same Apple Account at no extra cost.
