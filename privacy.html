Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Summit: Pot & Hammer Climb ("Summit") is a game made by an independent developer based in Georgia. In short: Summit does not collect any personal data.

What the game stores

Your player name and country (if you enter them), your progress, best times, race results and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No data is sent over the internet; the game works fully offline.
  • We do not sell or share any data.

Purchases

Summit is a paid app. The purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Summit collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
