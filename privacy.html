Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 (updated for online races)

Summit: Pot & Hammer Climb ("Summit") is a game made by an independent developer based in Georgia. This page explains what data the game uses. In short: only your player name, country and game results are sent, so that leaderboards and online races work.

What stays on your device

Your climbing progress, settings and race history are saved on your device.

What is sent to our server

  • Player name and country that you choose in the game, shown to other players on the leaderboards and in races.
  • Game results: your best height in the tower, your race rating, number of races and wins, and your best race time.
  • A random player ID created by the game on your device, so the leaderboard keeps one row per player. It is not linked to your Apple Account, device ID, email or any other identifier.
  • During an online race: your pot's position on the track, sent live to your opponent so they can see you. It is not stored.

The server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). We do not store IP addresses. Names that contain offensive words are replaced with "Climber".

What the game does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No chat or messages between players.
  • We do not sell or share any data.

Climbing the tower works fully offline. If you never connect, nothing is sent.

Purchases

Summit is a paid app. The purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. To remove your leaderboard row, email us your player name and we will delete it.

Children

Summit does not knowingly collect personal data from children. A player name can be any nickname; please don't use your real full name.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
